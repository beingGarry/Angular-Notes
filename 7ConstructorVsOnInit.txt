— Constructor vs ngOnInit()

What is the diff bet’n the Constructor and ngOnInit()

Constructor and ngOnInit seem the same, but they are called at diff phases.

Angular has two phases :

Bootstrapping phase
Change detection phase

Constructor :

The constructor is called at Bootstrapping phase.
This phase is when angular create the instances of services, pipe, components and directives in our module.
Angular initializes the component and resolves its dependencies and passes it to the constructor.

The change detection phase happens after the bootstrapping phase. This is mechanism where angular detects change in state of a component/directive and update the DOM to reflect as such.

Angular detect this using zone.js

At the change detection phase, these Hooks are called in sequence.

Oninit
Docheck
Onchanges.
Page 27 — ngOnInit Detailed

A constructor is automatically called when an instance of class is created. It initialized the properties of the class and does any other necessary setup.
It is used to inject dependencies, such as service and other component.
NgOnInit()

→ Is a lifecycle hook in angular that is called after the constructor is called and after the component input have been initialized.

It is used to perform any additional initialization that is required for a component.
ngOnit is commonly used for to call services or to setup subscriptions.
